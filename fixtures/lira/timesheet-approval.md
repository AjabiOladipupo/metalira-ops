# Lira test questions: timesheet approval and Work Units (slice 4.3)

Each request is what a manager might type, with the outcome Lira must reach (docs/LIRA.md). "Card" means a confirmation card; nothing changes until the person confirms. Capabilities are named as in `src/server/capabilities/registry.ts`. The period's counts are a read (`timesheets.review`, `agent: true`). Correcting a day (`timesheets.correct`) now has its preview (minutes now against after) and is `agent: true`: Lira shows a card with the preview and the person's reason. Approving, rejecting, "Approve N clean" and reopening (`timesheets.approve`, `timesheets.reject`, `timesheets.approve_clean`, `timesheets.reopen`) are `agent: false`: approving is a person's decision, so Lira explains and links to /app/timesheets. Work Units carry minutes, never money: billing is Phase 6.

Coastline's Gate A · Day runs 07:00 to 19:00 at Apapa Warehouse 3 on a manned-post contract. Today is Saturday 21 Nov 2026; the pay period is 20 Nov to 19 Dec 2026. On Friday 20 Nov Musa Bello clocked in at 07:00 and out at 19:00 (clean), Ngozi Eze clocked in at 07:02 with no clock-out, Amaka Udo was clocked in and out by her supervisor Tunde Bakare (no selfie, no location: a crew clock-in), and Yusuf Bello's clock-in was sent 3 hours after the time it claims. Halima Sani reports to the line manager Chidi Obi.
