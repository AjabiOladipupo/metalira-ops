# Lira test questions: publishing rosters and worker shifts

Each request is what a manager (or a worker) might type, with the outcome Lira must reach (docs/LIRA.md). Capabilities are named as in `src/server/capabilities/registry.ts`. Publishing a week (`roster.publish`, many rows, tells workers) has a preview (publish_roster's dry run) but is `agent: false` for now: Lira shows the preview and links to the Publish roster page, and a person presses Publish. Whether a week is published and what changed since (`roster.publication_status`) is a read, `agent: true`. A worker's own published shifts (`me.published_shifts`) are read in the worker app only (`agent: false`). Notices go to the worker app inbox and by email only (rule 8): never SMS, WhatsApp or push.

Apapa Warehouse 3 has Gate A · Day (06:00–18:00) and Gate A · Night (18:00–06:00), one guard each, every day. Today is Friday 20 Nov 2026; the week asked about is Monday 23 to Sunday 29 Nov. Version 1 of that week was published on 19 Nov at 14:05 with 12 people on shifts and 2 open. Since then Daniel Okafor was taken off Tuesday's night shift and Halima Bello was put on it.
