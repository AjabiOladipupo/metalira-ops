# Lira test questions: the roster

Each request is what a manager might type, with the outcome Lira must reach (docs/LIRA.md). "Card" means a confirmation card showing the capability's preview; nothing changes until the person confirms. Capabilities are named as in `src/server/capabilities/registry.ts`. Assigning and removing one person (`roster.assign`, `roster.remove_assignment`, kind `write`, one row) are `agent: true`. Copying last week (`roster.copy_last_week`, many rows) has a preview but is `agent: false` for now: Lira shows the preview and links to the Copy last week page. The week, the summary and suggestions are reads (`roster.week`, `roster.summary`, `roster.suggestions`, `agent: true`). The roster shows no money (roster cost and billable wait for Phase 4 and 7).

The Coastline contract is live at Apapa Warehouse 3 with Gate A · Day (06:00–18:00), Gate A · Night (18:00–06:00) and Patrol · Night (18:00–06:00), one guard each, every day. Today is Friday 20 Nov 2026; the week asked about is Monday 23 to Sunday 29 Nov. Yusuf Bello is mapped to Apapa with 36 h this week; Kunle Ade is in the relief pool (no site); Peter Adams leaves on 25 Nov.
