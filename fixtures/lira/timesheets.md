# Lira test questions: timesheet days (slice 4.2)

Each request is what a manager might type, with the outcome Lira must reach (docs/LIRA.md). "Card" means a confirmation card; nothing changes until the person confirms. Capabilities are named as in `src/server/capabilities/registry.ts`. The period summary and the days are reads (`timesheets.summary`, `timesheets.days`, `agent: true`). Correcting one day (`timesheets.correct`, kind `write`, one row) is `agent: false` until slice 4.3 adds its preview (current against new minutes): Lira explains what the correction would do and links to the day; the person makes it. It always needs the person's reason. Approving and rejecting days arrive in slice 4.3; bulk approval will be `agent: false`. Timesheets carry minutes, never money: pay is Phase 5, billing Phase 6.

Today is Saturday 21 Nov 2026, so the pay period is 20 Nov to 19 Dec 2026. Halima Sani reports to the line manager Chidi Obi.
